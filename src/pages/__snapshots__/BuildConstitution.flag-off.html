The assignment

A founding document for how you use AI

Every nation’s founders wrote down what they believed before they had to act on it. You’re invited to do the same for AI: draft a short personal constitution that states how you will — and won’t — use AI in your learning, work, and life.

Your constitution doubles as a harness. Paste it into the custom instructions of the AI tools you use, and it shapes how they respond to you: when to push back, when to ask you to do the thinking, when to cite sources.

1

Written constitution

One article for each of the five guiding principles, written as rules you can follow and an AI can apply.

2

Video explanation

A 30–120 second video on why you wrote your constitution and how you built it, including how you used AI.

Need a starting point? Draft your articles in the Constitution Builder in Learn, Module 2, then download and refine them.
