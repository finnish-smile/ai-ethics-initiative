Stewardship

As society continues to adapt to the ever-evolving technology of AI, it is important to remember that we are each stewards of our individual use of it and responsible for how it affects us, those around us, and the world we live in.

The purpose of this guidebook is not to give you a list of rules for how you should use AI, but rather to provide five principles that will sharpen the wise and ethical judgment you bring to it. Each principle is broken into three parts:

01 Principle A definition of what we are trying to safeguard.
02 Pause A question you can ask yourself when you’re uncertain about how to best use AI.
03 Practice Examples of applying the principle and the pause. Each practice includes examples of AI misuse and AI wise use.

Principle 01 of 05

Agency

You are free to choose

{{ agencyVerseText }}

{{ agencyVerseRef }}

Principle

Agency is the God-given gift to choose for yourself1 how to act and what to believe. AI can easily “diminish and suffocate [this] moral agency”2 when you allow it to make decisions or form opinions for you. Letting AI inform you is wise3. Letting it take the reins in your life is not. You are an agent to act and not to be acted upon4—not by any other person or technology. Cherish and protect your opportunities to use this gift. 

  1. Moses 3:17. churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures.
  2. David A. Bednar, “Things as They Really Are 2.0” (Worldwide Devotional for Young Adults, November 3, 2024), churchofjesuschrist.org/study/broadcasts.
  3. “Letting AI inform you is wise,” YouTube Short, youtube.com/shorts.
  4. 2 Nephi 2:26. churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures.

Pause

Am I making this decision, or allowing AI to make it for me?

Before moving forward, pause to reflect on how you use AI. Are there situations where you have given it your agency?

Practice

Here is one example of how AI can both aid and inhibit your decision making. Be mindful that in any given situation, the wisest use of AI may be no use at all. Talk to people you trust, ask for heavenly guidance, or contemplate on your own the decisions you need to make.

Scenario

You are uncertain about what to do with your future.

✕ Misuse

“Decide my future for me.”

What should I study? Where should I live? Should I marry this person? When should I have children?
✓ Wise use

“Help me understand my options so I can decide.”

Help me explore majors and schools. What career options might I be overlooking? What questions should I ask myself before making this decision? Help me compare these two choices.

You make a mistake at work or in a relationship

✕ Misuse

“Tell me what is right and wrong.”

Hover to see examples
  • What should I say to make this better? Generate an apology.
  • Help me explain why this isn't my fault.
  • Did I actually do something wrong?

Outsourcing moral judgment to AI can weaken your own conscience and growth.

✓ Wise use

“This is discernment fuel.”

Hover to see examples
  • How can I learn from this mistake?
  • How can I go about taking responsibility for this mistake? What questions can I ask others to understand their point of view?
  • How can I go about finding solutions?

Working through a mistake with AI can sharpen your judgment and sense of responsibility.

You are deciding your political views

✕ Misuse

“Just tell me what to think.”

Hover to see examples
  • Who should I vote for?
  • What is correct? What is wrong?
  • Figure out my ideology.
  • What should I think about this?

Letting AI dictate your political views can weaken your ability to reason and choose for yourself.

✓ Wise use

“Challenge my thinking, not replace it.”

Hover to see examples
  • Here is what I think about X, give me the strongest objection to it.
  • Many people believe X. Help me see where they are coming from.

Using AI to test and refine your views strengthens your own thinking.

Principle 02 of 05

Becoming

Refinement requires hardship

{{ becomingVerseText }}

{{ becomingVerseRef }}

Principle

Becoming is a process of personal growth that extends far beyond any grade or diploma; it is apart of an education for eternity1. With AI, it has become easy to bypass this growth when we use it to bypass meaningful effort. Like any algorithm, it also treats errors as a flaw to be corrected. You are not an algorithm; your weaknesses2 and efforts can be a catalyst for profound change3. In situations where you can choose to offload your work or errors onto a machine, remember that the objective is not producing, presenting, or perfecting, but rather working and becoming who God intends for you to become4.

Pause

Am I bypassing effort for an easy answer? Have I tried this myself first?

Before moving forward, pause to reflect on how you use AI. Are there situations where you have missed out on becoming a better person because you let AI do it for you?

Practice

Before you go to AI for help towards something you are learning, try it yourself first. Sometimes, the best use is no use. Be mindful when to use and not use AI; talk to friends, family, professors, mentors, or spend time by yourself to sit and work through problems. 

Scenario

You are behind on some difficult coursework

✕ Misuse

"Write this paper for me."

Show me all the steps to solving this problem. What are the key points in this reading? Make my writing sound better.
✓ Wise use

"Help me understand this so I can write it myself."

Teach me this concept, give me some practice problems. Where is my argument weak in this paper? Have I made any blatant mistakes? Here is what I got from this reading, did I miss anything? I can't solve this problem. Help me figure out where I am getting stuck.

You are working on a creative venture

✕ Misuse

"Tell me what you think of this — or just make it for me."

  • Generate lyrics/a story for me on this topic.

Letting AI originate your creative work can quiet your own voice before it has a chance to develop.

"Help me strengthen what I've already made."

Principle 03 of 05

Fellowship

I am because we are

{{ fellowshipVerseText }}

{{ fellowshipVerseRef }}

Principle

Fellowship is determined by intentional relationships with others. These relationships are at the core of your student experiences1and are one of the strongest predictors of a happy and healthy life2. Technology, and now AI, can act as a frictionless supplement for human interactions3; it never judges or tires—but remember that it also never asks anything of you. People, who do judge and tire, can't compete with the ease of turning to that technology, but their needs will grow you and your needs will grow them4. Be mindful of how you use AI and if your use of it is keeping you from strengthening your connections with those whom God has placed in your life.

Pause

Will my AI use strengthen or weaken my relationships with others?

Before moving forward, pause to reflect on how you use AI. Do you use it as a supplement for friend, family, teachers, or God?

Practice

What you take to a machine, take to a person. Sometimes, the best use is no use. Be mindful when to use and not use AI. Talk to friends, family, professors, mentors, or spend time by yourself to sit and contemplate decisions you need to make.

Scenario

You are going through something hard and need advice.

✕ Misuse

“Just tell me what to do. I don’t want to bother anyone.”

I’m really struggling. What should I do? Talk me through this so I don’t have to tell anyone. Be the one I come to when things get hard. Write what I should say so I don’t have to explain myself.
✓ Wise use

“Help me find the right people to turn to.”

Who in my life might understand what I’m going through? Help me find the words to ask a friend for help. How do I bring this up with my family? What campus resources could support me right now?

Principle 04 of 05

Discernment

Test the truth before you trust it

{{ discernmentVerseText }}

{{ discernmentVerseRef }}

Principle

Discernment is the ability to distinguish truth from error, good from bad, and wise from foolish. Generative AI is making it increasingly difficult to mark these distinctions both in its unintentional hallucinations and in people using it to intentionally deceive others. Today we can still often tell what is AI and what isn't1, but one day we may not be able to. If you can't outright tell if something is real, wise, or good, pause to think through it, fact-check what you can, and listen to the spirit of truth2. You have been given a prophetic promise to discern truth from error3, so remain critical of the world's "truths" and open to the spirit, and you will be blessed to see things as they really are4.

  2. John 16:13. churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures.
  3. Russell M. Nelson, "Overcome the World and Find Rest," Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, October 2022, churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference.
  4. Doctrine and Covenants 93:24, churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures.

Do I know this information is real or true? How?

Before you move forward, pause and reflect on how you use AI. Are you fact checking the information it gives you?

Principle 05 of 05

Integrity

Be true at all times

{{ integrityVerseText }}

{{ integrityVerseRef }}

Principle

UNFINISHED DRAFT
Integrity is transparency, consistency, honesty, and admitting mistakes. When you make a commitment on how to use AI, stick to it. In difficulty, endure it. In uncertainty, ask. In error, confess. Be true to yourself at all times — in how you use it at school and in your personal life. No one is looking over your shoulder to make sure you are using it right, and no one will be grading or judging how you use it 100% of the time. It is up to you. Examine how you use AI. Do you show integrity?
